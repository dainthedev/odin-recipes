Olde-timey pizza

This is the recipe I used to start my pizza business and grocery store with. I removed the secret ingredient though. I will take it to my grave.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • yeast
  • salt
  • Sugar
  • Water
  • Pizza sauce
  • Cheese
  • Whatever other stuff you want on top

Steps

  • Do the yeast bowl thing
  • Mix it with the flour and stuff
  • Mix it up
  • Stretch it out onto a floured surface in the shape of a pizza
  • Put it on a pan
  • Cover it with the sauce
  • Shred the cheese and sprinke it on top of the dough
  • Apply all other toppings as you see fit.
  • Bake for 10 minutes at 400 degrees
  • Remove pizza, slice and eat.
